How TermRadar works

1

Read

PDFs are converted to text right in your browser with PDF.js. Tables, week-by-week outlines and paragraph-style syllabi are all supported.

2

Extract

A rule engine finds every real date and grading weight. Optionally, a 1.5B-parameter language model (Qwen 2.5 via WebLLM) runs on your GPU to understand messy wording.

3

Verify

AI proposes, rules verify. An AI-suggested deadline is kept only if its date actually appears in your syllabus, so hallucinated dates never reach your calendar.

4

Plan

Deadlines are scored by type and grade weight, stacked across courses into a heatmap, and turned into "start by" dates, a study-coach plan and a calendar file.
